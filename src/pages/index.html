What does a cow's history look like when she changes hands?

When we bought a cow from another farmer, her whole record reached us as a WhatsApp photo of one notebook page. Her mother was Tag 502. She was 75 percent Friesian. She peaked at 10 litres at one milking.

We could not check any of it. So we built a record that can be checked.

How it works

One record per cow, kept every day

Milk

Litres for every cow at every milking, morning and evening.

Breeding

Heat, service and calving, with a reminder when each is due.

Health

Treatments and vaccines, with the date her milk is safe again.

Your team

Each worker records on their own phone. You see who did what.

No network needed. Records save on the phone and sync when the network returns. A missed signal at 5am costs you nothing.

Who owns a cow's history?

You do.

Every entry is kept, and a correction is added as a new entry, never written over. When you sell a cow, her record can go with her, and a buyer, vet or lender can see what she has done.
