Privacy

How we handle your details

This notice covers the octash.co website. It explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us to change or delete it. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

Octash Labs Ltd., Oyo State, Nigeria, is responsible for the personal data collected on this website. You can reach us at contact@octash.co or on WhatsApp at 0808 109 4648.

What we collect

When you fill in the "Save your place" form, we collect:

  • your full name and phone number
  • your email address, if you give it
  • whether you are a farmer, farm manager or partner organisation
  • your state and town, and the number of cows on your farm

When you visit the site, Google Tag Manager and our analytics tools record how the site is used, such as the pages viewed, the type of device and browser, and the general location from your IP address. This may use cookies.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you about joining Octash and to onboard your farm or organisation.
  • To plan the first release, for example by knowing which states and herd sizes have signed up.
  • To understand how the website is used and make it better.

We process form details with your consent, which you give by sending the form. You can withdraw it at any time. We process site usage data in our legitimate interest in running a working website.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your details. We share them only with the service providers that run this website for us: Cloudflare (hosting and form handling), Resend (delivering form submissions to our inbox) and Google (site analytics). Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria, under safeguards the Act permits. We will also share details if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep form details for as long as we are in touch with you about Octash, and for no more than 24 months after our last contact. Then we delete them. If you become an Octash user, your farm records are covered by the app's own terms.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, delete them, or stop using them. You can also withdraw your consent. Send your request to contact@octash.co or WhatsApp 0808 109 4648. We will answer within 30 days.

If you are not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
